Why arsenic testing matters

What a district groundwater study measured.

What was measured

A 2025 study tested 28 groundwater samples from water schemes in Gujranwala District. About 64% exceeded the WHO guideline of 10 µg/L for arsenic. The small study describes those sources, and its results cannot be applied to every local source or finished bottle.

Look for an arsenic result

Arsenic can occur in groundwater. Appearance, taste and a TDS meter cannot measure it. Ask for the laboratory’s arsenic result in µg/L, the date and the applicable limit.

Match the batch

Find the code on your bottle and use it to locate the finished-water report. If the report does not match, ask the supplier which batch was tested.
