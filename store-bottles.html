How to store 19-litre bottles

Keep sealed refills shaded, clean and upright.

Choose a clean place

Stand sealed bottles upright on a clean rack or stable raised surface, away from direct sun, heat, cleaning chemicals and floor splashes. Rotate your stock so older sealed bottles are used first.

Keep the seal intact

Check for damage and make sure the cap is intact before fitting the bottle. Keep the neck clean when handling it, and follow your supplier’s storage and use instructions once opened.

Set empties aside

Keep empties upright and separate from dirty containers. Put the cap back on if practical. Let the supplier know how many you have for collection and set aside any visibly damaged bottles.
