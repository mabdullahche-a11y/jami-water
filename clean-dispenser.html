How to clean a water dispenser

A routine for the tap, tray and reservoir.

Start with the maker’s manual

Check the cleaning instructions for your model. Unplug it, let hot parts cool and remove the bottle before draining. Wash your hands before handling parts that touch the water.

Clean, then sanitise

Wash removable parts as the manual directs. Use the sanitiser, contact time and rinse procedure specified by the manufacturer. Keep cleaning agents separate and avoid pouring unapproved products into the reservoir.

Fit the next bottle

Wash your hands again. Keep the cap and bottle neck clear of the counter, and check the seal before fitting. Wipe the drip tray regularly and note when you last cleaned the reservoir.
